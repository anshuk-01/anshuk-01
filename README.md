# Hi, I'm Anshu Singh 👋

**Data Engineer | Cloud Data Pipelines | Snowflake · Airflow · AWS**

I'm a B.Tech graduate and Data Engineer who builds and maintains reliable, production-grade data pipelines. My work centers on integrating **cross-regional data** into Snowflake, orchestrating workflows with Airflow on AWS, and delivering analytics-ready datasets to business users through Power BI.

I care about pipelines that are dependable, observable, and easy to maintain, and I enjoy the ML side of data as well.

---

## 🔭 What I Do

- 🌍 Build and maintain ETL/ELT pipelines ingesting data from multiple regions into a central warehouse
- ⚙️ Orchestrate, schedule, and monitor workflows with **Apache Airflow**
- ❄️ Model, load, and optimize data in **Snowflake**
- ☁️ Work with AWS services (**S3, CloudWatch, VPC**) for storage, monitoring, and secure networking
- 📊 Deliver dashboards and reports in **Power BI**, and automate business workflows with **Power Automate**
